Programs

Three tracks. One outcome: real fluency and real confidence.

Every program is a live, 3-week micro-track mapped to global frameworks like CEFR and IELTS — built for busy students and professionals, not classrooms.

Test Prep · 3-Week Micro-Track

IELTS Preparation

Intensive strategies for IELTS Academic/General, designed to push students toward Band 7.0 and above for university admissions and scholarships.

  • Writing Task 1 & 2 mastery
  • Speaking & fluency drills
  • Reading & listening technique
  • Timed mock exams with feedback
  • Live Zoom coaching throughout
Academic Prep · Live Cohort

IGCSE Preparation

Structured, subject-focused revision and exam technique coaching for students preparing for core IGCSE examinations.

  • Core subject exam strategy
  • Structured revision planning
  • Past-paper practice
  • Exam technique coaching
  • Personalized mentorship
Language Skills · 3-Week Micro-Track

Conversation Fluency

Live speaking practice built for natural, confident everyday and workplace conversation — plus accent confidence coaching.

  • Real-time speaking practice
  • Accent & pronunciation confidence
  • Everyday & workplace scenarios
  • Small, live cohorts
  • Certificate of completion
