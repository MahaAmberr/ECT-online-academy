Why ECTS

What guides everything we teach.

Our Vision

Confidence that unlocks opportunity

To transform our students' confidence and unlock global academic and corporate opportunities under Saudi Vision 2030 and beyond.

Our Mission

Local talent, global standards

To provide accessible, interactive and structured live training that bridges local talent with global standards — helping every student achieve standardized English fluency, master modern digital tools, and earn verifiable credentials through engaging 3-week micro-tracks and personalized mentorship.

Core Pillars

The principles behind every live session.

These aren't wall posters — they're what we measure ourselves against, from curriculum design to how every mentor teaches.

Live, Human-Centered Learning

No boring pre-recorded lectures. Every class is hosted live on Zoom, with real-time speaking practice, active Q&A and personalized feedback.

Standardized Excellence

Our tracks are mapped to recognized global frameworks — including CEFR and official IELTS assessment criteria — so your skills are respected worldwide.

Practical & Unbundled

We respect your schedule. Our 3-week intensive micro-courses focus purely on actionable skills — from essay speed to interview technique.

Verifiable Credentials

Every graduate receives an official Certificate of Completion with a unique QR code for instant employer verification.
